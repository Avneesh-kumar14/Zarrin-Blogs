# 🎉 Dashboard Features Update - Now Complete!

## ✅ What's Been Added to Your Dashboard

Your dashboard now has **5 main navigation items** in the sidebar:

### **1. Dashboard** 📊
- Location: `/dashboard/analytics`
- View your blog statistics and analytics
- See overview of your activity

### **2. My Blogs** 📝
- Location: `/dashboard/myblogs`
- View all your published blogs
- Edit or delete existing posts
- See blog performance metrics

### **3. Categories** 🏷️
- Location: `/dashboard/categories`
- Manage blog categories
- Organize your content

### **4. My Drafts** ✍️ **[NEW]**
- Location: `/dashboard/drafts`
- View all your draft blogs
- Edit draft button - continue writing
- Delete button - remove unwanted drafts
- Create new draft button
- See last updated date for each draft
- Beautiful animated header with gradient design

### **5. My Profile** 👤 **[NEW]**
- Location: `/dashboard/profile`
- View your complete user profile
- See your bio and avatar
- View your statistics:
  - **Total blogs written**
  - **Followers count**
  - **Following count**
- See list of your published blogs
- Professional gradient design

## 🔐 Special Features in My Profile

### **When viewing your own profile in dashboard:**
✅ No "Follow" button (you can't follow yourself)

### **When viewing another user's profile (public):**

✅ Their published blogs  
✅ Follower count  
✅ Following count

## 📍 How to Access Dashboard

### **From anywhere on the site:**
1. Login to your account
2. Click on your avatar/name in the navbar
3. You'll be directed to the dashboard

### **Direct URLs:**
- Dashboard Home: `http://localhost:3000/dashboard/analytics`
- My Blogs: `http://localhost:3000/dashboard/myblogs`
- Categories: `http://localhost:3000/dashboard/categories`
- My Drafts: `http://localhost:3000/dashboard/drafts`
- My Profile: `http://localhost:3000/dashboard/profile`
